What each number means

Leads
People who filled in a form from that source. If someone comes in twice, the first source keeps the credit.
Qualified
Leads the AI check marked as a good fit (budget, B2B, decision maker, timeline).
Booked
Leads who booked a sales call through the calendar link.
Showed
Leads who actually turned up to the call.
First reply
Average minutes between the lead opting in and our first message reaching them. Lower is better; under 5 minutes is the goal.
Cost per lead
Spend for that source ÷ number of leads.
Cost per booked call
Spend ÷ booked calls. This is the number that decides the winner.
Cost per show
Spend ÷ calls that actually happened. The truest cost of a sales conversation.
No-show rate
Of the calls that were due, the share where the lead did not turn up.
Spend
Money spent on that source in the date range: Meta ads, Google ads for the website, and referral commissions and gift cards.
